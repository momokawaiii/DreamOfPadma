# Content Rules

- Chinese companion for user reading: `Content/AGENTS.zh-CN.md`

- Project-owned assets go under `Content/Padma/`.
- Vendor or marketplace assets go under `Content/ThirdParty/` and are not edited in place.
- Experimental assets go under `Content/Sandbox/` and are not referenced by shipping content.
- Follow `Docs/Content/Naming.md`.
- Use stable IDs in data assets; do not use a display name as an identity.
- Move or rename UE assets through the Unreal Editor and verify redirectors afterward.
